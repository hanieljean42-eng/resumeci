- 7,850= seven thousand, eight hundred and fifty

- To buy : to give money and obtain an object.

II- LANGUAGE FUNCTION: comparing clothes ( comparer les vêtements)
Pour comparer des vêtements, l’on utilise deux (02) types d’adjectif : adjectif
court (Ex. : cheap) ou adjectif long ( Ex. : expensive).
Example1: the shirt is cheaper than the jeans.
Example 2: the jeans are more expensive than the shirt.
