TEACHING POINTS
VOCABULARY
Adverbs of frequency:
Always – sometimes – never
– often – on Fridays
LANGUAGE FUNCTION
Expressing frequency
STRUCTURES
We always start class at O’clock.
We never speak Spanish in class.
INPUT PHASE
Vocabulary
Always = all the time
Ex: I always take a bath before going to school.
Sometimes = on some occasions
Ex: Yao sometimes has class at 8.
Often = frequently, many times
Never = not at any time
