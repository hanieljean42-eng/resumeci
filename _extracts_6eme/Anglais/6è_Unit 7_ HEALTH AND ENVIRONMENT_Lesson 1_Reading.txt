Situation d’évaluation 1(Communication activity)
Lors d’un concours de lecture organisé au Collège Municipal de Koro par
l’Ambassade des Etats-Unis, les élèves de la 6è exploitent un texte pour en
comprendre le contenu. Faisant partie de ces élèves, réponds aux questions qui suivent
en lisant le texte ci-dessous.
Hygiene is very important. To be healthy, these are many things we must do.
For example, we must take at least one shower every day. We must brush our teeth and
drink safe water from a tap. There many things we must not do. For example, we must
not wear dirty clothes, drink water from the river and eat with dirty hands. We must
keep our body clean. Our body is a fantastic machine. We must always protect it.
