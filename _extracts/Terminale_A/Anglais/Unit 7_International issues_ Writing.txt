I. LEARNING CONTEXT
During their English class, the students of TleA1 of Lycée Moderne GAF Touba are writing to express
their opinions about the extinction of some languages.
II. LESSON CONTENT
Writing strategy for an opinion essay
- Paragraph 1: Introduction: stating your position
- Paragraph 2: reason 1 for supporting your view
- Paragraph 3: reason 2 for supporting your view
- Paragraph 4: a few points supporting the opposite opinion and reason 3 for supporting your
view
- Paragraph 5: Conclusion: restating your position
III. COMMUNICATION ACTIVITY
You are participating in an essay writing competition organized by the British embassy on the
following topic “Children should not be prevented from speaking their mother tongue at
school.”
In your essay,
-say whether you agree or not with the statement;
- support your position with arguments and examples.
(25 lines maximum)
