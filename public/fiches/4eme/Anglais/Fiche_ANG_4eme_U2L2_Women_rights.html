II. Expressing rights — have the right to

Women have the right to vote / express their opinions / take part in elections.
Boys don't have the right to insult girls. · My father doesn't have the right to beat my mother.

III. Expressing obligations — have the duty to / must / have to

She has the duty to study her lessons. · Women must provide children with a good education. · Men mustn't rape women. · Students have to study every day. · Traders don't have to be dishonest.
must / have to / have the duty to = obligation ; mustn't = interdiction ; don't have to = pas d'obligation.
⚠️ Pièges classiques :
  • mustn't = interdit ≠ don't have to = pas obligé.
  • 3ᵉ personne : She has the right/duty to…
  • Right = ce qu'on peut faire ; duty = ce qu'on doit faire — ne pas inverser.
