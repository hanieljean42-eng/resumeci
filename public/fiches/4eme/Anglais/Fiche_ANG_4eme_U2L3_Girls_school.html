📝 FICHE DE RÉSUMÉ

📖 Anglais🏫 4èmeUnit 2 Lesson 3 : Girls must go to school too! — opinion & comparaisons

I. Vocabulary — Girls' schooling

to be successful (réussir) · to graduate (obtenir un diplôme) · a housewife (femme au foyer) · to discriminate (traiter différemment) ≠ equal treatment · to accomplish an ambition · clever/bright/smart (intelligent) · to be skilled · to win a scholarship · early marriage · independent

II. Expressing an opinion

In my opinion, … / I think (that) … / For me, … / From my point of view, … + girls can be successful at school.

III. Making comparisons

1. Comparative of superiority

Adjectif court : adj + -er + than → some girls are brighter than some boys.
Adjectif long : more + adj + than → girls are more successful than boys.

2. Comparative of equality

as + adjectif + as → Girls are as skilled as boys. / as intelligent as boys.
Règle : adjectif 1 syllabe → -er ; adjectif long (2+ syllabes) → more … than ; égalité → as … as.
⚠️ Pièges classiques :
  • more bright than ❌ → brighter than ✅ (adjectif court).
  • Ne jamais mélanger : as … than ❌ → as … as ✅.
  • opinion ≠ comparison : « In my opinion » pour donner un avis ; comparatif pour comparer.
