II. Expressing obligation — have got to / must / have to

People have got to practice peace.
Sally has got to practice tolerance.
I must be a peace-maker. · Ivoirians have to be peace-makers. · Sonia has to be a peace-maker.
3 façons d'exprimer l'obligation :
  • have got to + BV → has got to à la 3ᵉ personne
  • must + BV (invariable)
  • have to + BV → has to à la 3ᵉ personne
⚠️ Pièges classiques :
  • 3ᵉ personne : has got to / has to — mais must ne change jamais.
  • Après got to / must / have to → base verbale sans to.
  • Vocabulaire des quiz : reconciliation = groups friendly again after conflicts ; forgiveness = refusing to be angry.
