📖 Anglais🏫 4èmeUnit 3 Lesson 1 : How do you go to school? — transport

I. Vocabulary — Means of transport

a bicycle · a car · a plane · a boat · a canoe · a train
Verbe associéExemple
to drivea car / a bus
to ridea bicycle / a motorbike
to flya plane / a helicopter
to pilota train
to paddlea canoe
to goon foot

II. Expressing movement — on / by / in

How do you go to school? → I go to school on foot. / by car. / by plane. / by train.
I am in a car. · I am on a bus.
Règle : by + moyen de transport (sans article) · on foot (à pied) · in a car (fermé, petit) · on a bus/train/boat (grand véhicule).

III. Expressing wishes — want to / would like to

How do you want to go to school? → I want to go by car. = I would like to go by car.
would like to = plus poli que want to ; les deux sont suivis de la base verbale (to + BV).
⚠️ Pièges classiques :
  • by car (pas « by a car ») ; in a car / on a bus (avec article).
  • on foot — jamais « by foot ».
  • Associés figés : ride a bicycle, drive a car, paddle a canoe.
