📝 FICHE DE RÉSUMÉ

📖 Anglais🏫 4èmeUnit 4 Lesson 1 : What would you like to wear? — fashion

I. Vocabulary — Fashion

fashion (la mode) · fashionable (à la mode) ≠ old-fashioned (démodé) · clothes (vêtements) · to wear (porter) · to wash (laver) · hairstyles (Afro, Punk, dreadlocks) · hairdresser (coiffeur/euse) · shoemaker (cordonnier) · a pair of sneakers · to give up (abandonner)

II. Present simple vs Present continuous

Present simple (habitude)Present continuous (en cours)
I wear a jacket every day.
Ivorian students wear uniforms.		Look! Seka is wearing a nice polo today.
Today I am putting on new clothes.
Marqueurs : present simple → every day, usually, always ; present continuous → Look! now, today, at the moment. Structure : be + V-ing.

III. Expressing opinions on fashion

What do you think of fashion? / What's your opinion about fashion?
→ I think that fashion is not always good for young people.
→ In my opinion, fashion is not always good for young people.
→ From my point of view, …
⚠️ Pièges classiques :
  • wear = porter (vêtements) ; put on = mettre (action).
  • 3ᵉ pers. au présent simple : he wears.
  • old-fashioned = démodé ≠ fashionable.
