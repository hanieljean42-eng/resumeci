📖 Anglais🏫 4èmeUnit 3 Lesson 3 : My new life in the UK — reported speech & past simple

I. Vocabulary — Living abroad

to stand for (signifier) · to settle in (s'installer) · to start (commencer) · to travel (voyager) · a scholarship (bourse) · means of transport · foreign (étranger) · to make out (comprendre) · kingdom · palace · rude ≠ respectful

II. Reporting what someone said — indirect speech

Direct speechIndirect speech
“I always visit London with my parents”, Kadi said.Kadi said that she always visited London with her parents.
“I am the first black student in this college”, he told me.He told me that he was the first black student in that college.
Changements au discours indirect : présent → prétérit (visit→visited, am→was, like→liked) ; pronoms adaptés (I→she/he, we→they) ; this→that, here→there ; verbes introducteurs : said / told me / informed / wanted to know.

III. Expressing past actions — past simple

Kofi was in 5e last year. · My father arrived in London in 1990. · Two weeks ago, I went to my village.
Marqueurs du passé : last year, in 1990, … ago, yesterday. Verbe + -ed (réguliers) ou forme irrégulière (go→went, get→got, be→was/were, make→made).
⚠️ Pièges classiques :
  • Indirect speech : recul d'un temps après said/told.
  • told me/us/them (avec objet) vs said (sans objet).
  • « I don't like it » → she informed them that she didn't like it.
