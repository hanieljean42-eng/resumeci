II. Expressing an opinion — I think…

— Do you think it is important to keep our school clean?
— Yes, it is important. I think we need to create an Environment Club.
I think we must throw waste in the dustbin.
Opinion : I think + phrase. Question : Do you think (that)…?

III. Making suggestions — Why don't you/we…?

— I am suffocating. → Why don't you open the windows?
— Our classroom is dirty. → Why don't we clean it?
— I don't feel well. → Why don't you go to the hospital?
Suggestion : Why don't you/we + base verbale + …? Réponse polie : Thank you for your suggestion.
Why don't you… (conseil à l'autre) / Why don't we… (action commune).

IV. Reading — Hygiene and health

L'hygiène participe à la prévention des maladies. Avoir les mains propres donne fierté, confiance, confort et dignité à la maison comme en public (self-esteem). L'hygiène concerne aussi l'environnement : chaque maison devrait avoir une poubelle extérieure avec couvercle hermétique contre insectes et pluie ; un sac plastique en liner facilite l'évacuation des ordures.
