II. Present perfect vs Past simple

Present perfect — action récente/liée au présentPast simple — action terminée, datée
The tailor has already made your shirt.
We have just bought new clothes.
Sanogo has not set up his business yet.		This artist wore a wig last week.
Aya bought a dress two weeks ago.
Yao went to his farm yesterday.
Present perfect = have/has + participe passé ; marqueurs : already, just, yet, never, ever.
Past simple = verbe au passé ; marqueurs : yesterday, last week, …ago, in 1960.
⚠️ Pièges classiques :
  • Avec yesterday / last year / ago → past simple, jamais present perfect.
  • yet → phrase négative/interrogative en fin : hasn't … yet.
  • Prétérits irréguliers : wear→wore, buy→bought, make→made, go→went.
