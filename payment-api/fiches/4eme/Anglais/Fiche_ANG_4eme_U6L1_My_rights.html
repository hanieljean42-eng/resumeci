II. Making requests — can

Can you tell me where to buy a stamp, please?
Can I get an identity card, please? / Can I have a stamp?
Structure : Can + sujet + base verbale + please (politesse). Ex : Can you show me your ID card?

III. Obligation & prohibition — must / mustn't

ObligationProhibition
Citizens must pay taxes. · Koffi must go to school. · People must obey the law.Children mustn't smoke cigarettes. · Citizens mustn't destroy public services.
must = obligation · mustn't = interdiction (forbidden). Équivalent : to be obliged to = must.
⚠️ Pièges classiques :
  • mustn't = interdit ≠ « pas obligé » (don't have to).
  • Après can/must/mustn't → base verbale sans to.
  • Droits vs devoirs : éducation, soins, protection = droits ; payer ses impôts, obéir à la loi = devoirs.
