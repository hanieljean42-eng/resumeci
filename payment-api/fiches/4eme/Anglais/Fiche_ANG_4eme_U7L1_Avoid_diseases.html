II. Giving advice — should / shouldn't

You should go to hospital when you are sick.
You shouldn't drink dirty water.
There's an accident on the highway → we should call an ambulance.
should + BV = conseil · shouldn't + BV = déconseiller. Équivalent : It is important to… / It is not good to…

III. Passive voice — be + past participle

Nina was bitten by a mosquito.
The patient is given a prescription by the doctor.
Ahmed was bitten by mosquitoes yesterday.
Passif : sujet + be (au même temps que le verbe actif) + participe passé (+ by + agent).
Actif → Passif : The doctor gave me a prescription → A prescription was given to me by the doctor. · The patient buys pills → Pills are bought by the patient.

IV. Reading text — Malaria

Malaria = serious disease in tropical areas, caused by infected mosquito bites. Symptoms (1 week–1 month after bite) : high fever, chills, headache. Detection : temperature + blood sample under a microscope. Treatment : medicines exist. Prevention : keep environment clean + sleep under a mosquito net.
⚠️ Pièges classiques :
  • Passif : be s'accorde avec le nouveau sujet et prend le temps du verbe actif (bit → was bitten).
  • shouldn't + BV : you shouldn't drink (pas to drink).
  • chemistry en anglais CI = pharmacie (faux ami : pas « chimie »).
