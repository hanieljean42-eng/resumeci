II. Expressing feelings — feel + adjective

Children feel excited to go to the attraction park.
Kouao feels very proud — he produced 10 tons of cotton.
Farmers feel worried when it doesn't rain.

III. Asking questions

Wh- questions (réponse précise) : When did Côte d'Ivoire become a French colony? / Why did the French arrest Samory Touré?
Yes/No questions (réponse oui/non) : Do you know…? / Are you a student?
Prépositions utiles : in 2017 · grow from 3 million to 4.5 million · by 2020 (< em>reach) · develop along highways · during colonisation · within a few years.
⚠️ Pièges classiques :
  • feel + adjectif : I feel sad (pas « feel sadly »).
  • Wh- + did + sujet + BV : When did CI become…
  • Ordre des mots à la question : inversion (auxiliaire avant le sujet).
